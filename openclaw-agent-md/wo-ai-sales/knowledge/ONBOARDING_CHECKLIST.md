# ONBOARDING_CHECKLIST.md

Source: approved checklist `Onboarding Checklist.png`, dated local file approval 2026-07-31.

The checklist helps ensure AIChat has enough information to serve prospective Wedding Organizer clients well.

## Needed Documents And Data

1. Company profile and contact information: WO name, address, Instagram, website, WhatsApp, email.
2. How admin replies to customer chats: language style, address terms, emoji rules, greeting style.
3. Customer-service flow: first chat, consultation, offer, follow-up, booking.
4. Package and price list: Silver, Gold, Premium, or current package structure.
5. Package details: decoration, MC, MUA, documentation, catering, and other inclusions.
6. Partner venues: venue partners, service area, special prices.
7. Partner vendors: MUA, photographer, videographer, catering, entertainment.
8. Operating hours and consultation schedule.
9. Follow-up rules: when to follow up and when a lead is considered inactive.
10. Payment rules: DP, settlement, refund, reschedule.
11. When admin must contact human team: negotiation, complaint, owner decision.
12. Files allowed to send to customers: company profile, catalog, pricelist, PDF.
13. Customer FAQ.
14. How to create special price offers: discount, promo, custom package.
15. Document templates: quotation, invoice, contract, receipt.
16. Workflow after booking: DP until event day.
17. Event team job descriptions: WO, LO, runner, crew.
18. Event rundown templates: wedding, akad, reception, engagement.
19. Complaint and sensitive case SOP: refund, late vendor, force majeure.
20. Admin authority boundary: what admin may decide alone.
21. Customer data storage rules: privacy and data access.
22. Existing customer data: CRM, Google Sheet, Excel.
23. Work schedule and calendar: Google Calendar, reminders.
24. Example customer chats: censored chat examples.
25. How to update AI information: price, package, vendor, promo updates.

## Customer-Safe Summary

`Biasanya yang perlu disiapin itu profil WO, cara admin jawab chat, alur customer, paket-harga, vendor/venue, aturan pembayaran, FAQ, template dokumen, dan SOP operasional.`

## Boundary

Not every document must be complete on day one. If a WO does not have everything ready yet, say onboarding can start from the most important data first, then completed gradually with owner confirmation.
