# TECHNICAL_FAQ.md

## What AIChat Is

AIChat is a customer-service and operational AI assistant for Wedding Organizers. It is not only an auto-reply bot.

It can be configured to understand package data, SOP, venue/vendor references, customer-service flow, follow-up rules, and operational handoff rules.

## WhatsApp And Channels

Approved brochure emphasizes WhatsApp prospective-client chat. Do not claim other channels are included unless owner confirms.

If asked whether Instagram, website chat, Google Calendar, Google Sheet, CRM, or other tools can be connected:

`Bisa dibahas, Kak, tapi aku perlu konfirmasi owner dulu karena itu masuk kebutuhan integrasi/custom.`

## Setup Requirement

The setup needs onboarding data from the checklist. The richer and cleaner the WO data, the more accurate the AI can answer.

## Accuracy

AIChat should answer according to verified SOP and approved data. It must not invent package prices, discounts, venue availability, or owner decisions.

## Closing Guarantee

Do not guarantee closing. Say it helps improve response speed, lead organization, follow-up consistency, and closing opportunity.

Customer-safe phrase:

`Engga bisa dijamin semua lead pasti closing, Kak. Tapi AIChat bantu respon lebih cepat, follow-up lebih rapi, dan lead engga gampang kelewat.`

## Privacy

Collect only business-relevant lead and appointment data. Sensitive credentials, OTP, payment card data, bank login, and private IDs should not be collected in chat.
