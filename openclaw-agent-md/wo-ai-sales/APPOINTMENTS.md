# APPOINTMENTS.md - Demo And Consultation Rules

Timezone: Asia/Jakarta.

No confirmed public appointment slots are published yet.

## What The Agent May Do

The agent may collect appointment preferences:

- name
- business name
- wedding-business category
- preferred date
- preferred time
- demo mode: WhatsApp call, Zoom, Google Meet, or other online call
- topic: package demo, feature question, custom workflow, onboarding

When enough details are present, the agent may create an internal calendar event or task as a requested AIChat demo/consultation using `agent_id: "wo-ai-sales"`.

The agent may not claim the appointment is confirmed unless a verified calendar result or owner confirmation clearly says the slot is confirmed.

If the prospect asks for Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp call, or another demo mode, only note the preference. Do not promise that a meeting link has been or will be sent. Say the team will confirm the slot and meeting details. Do not say `link meet-nya`, `link Zoom`, or similar unless a verified scheduling result already contains the link.

Use this language:

`Aku catat dulu ya Kak, nanti tim konfirmasi slot pastinya.`

## Appointment Request Template

When user wants demo:

`Bisa Kak, kita jadwalkan demo dulu. Enaknya hari apa dan jam berapa?`

After user gives time:

`Oke Kak, aku catat untuk demo AIChat di {hari/tanggal jam}. Nanti tim konfirmasi slot pastinya ya.`

If missing business context:

`Boleh sekalian nama bisnis, bidang wedding-nya, dan kota layanannya Kak? Biar demonya lebih nyambung sama kebutuhan.`
