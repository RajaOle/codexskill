# Package Benefits Include

status: VERIFIED
last_updated: 2026-07-31
visibility: PUBLIC

## Customer-Facing Rules

- Use this file for general inclusion categories when the customer asks what Zahira Wedding packages usually include.
- Exact inclusions still follow the selected active package in `PACKAGES_AND_PRICING.md`.
- Do not promise every item for every package. Say inclusions depend on selected package, pax, venue, and final quotation.

## General Benefit Categories

### Dekorasi

- Pelaminan 6-12 meter depending on package.
- Kursi pelaminan.
- Mini garden.
- Mix artificial and fresh flower.
- Standing flower and standing lamp according to package tier.
- Karpet pelaminan/jalan and optional melamin according to package tier.
- Wedding gate.
- Gazebo.
- Standing lamp.
- Welcome sign.
- Akad table and chairs.
- Background penerima tamu.
- Janur.
- Kotak angpau.
- Meja penerima tamu.
- Hand bouquet.
- Backdrop music, penerima tamu, and photobooth on applicable tiers.
- Photo gallery/story line, black screen/starlight, and decorative trees on higher tiers.

### Catering

- Main buffet based on pax.
- Typical buffet choices include rice, fried rice/noodle/vermicelli, soup option, salad or vegetable option, fish, chicken, pudding, fruit, small cakes, juice or soft drink, crackers, and mineral water.
- Gubukan/stall menu options depend on pax and selected package.
- Free after-akad menu may be included depending on package.
- Catering decoration may include buffet decor, VIP buffet decor, dessert/initial decor, and gubukan decor depending on package.

### Makeup and Attire

- Akad makeup and hijab/hairdo.
- Reception touch-up.
- National akad attire for bride and groom.
- National reception attire for bride and groom.
- Accessories and melati.
- Parent, family, penerima tamu, and among tamu makeup/attire depend on package.
- Softlens, henna, or nail art may be included depending on package.

### Acara and Entertainment

- Professional MC for akad and reception.
- Live music with player/singer according to package.
- Sound system for akad and reception.
- Optional acoustic, mini chamber, or full-band upgrades.

### Photo and Video

- Photo and video coverage.
- One photographer and one videographer in the standard catalog package.
- Wedding book/album, printed frame, cinematic video, edited photos, and soft files according to package.

### Wedding Organizer On The Day

- Wedding planning booklet.
- Project Leader/Officer and WO crew on the day based on pax/package.
- Crew packages range from 3 crew up to 8-10 crew.
- Rundown, wedding booklet, vendor coordination, family meeting, and event timekeeping.
- WhatsApp coordination group.

### Common Free Items

- Hand bouquet and custom guest book according to package.
- Henna, nail art, normal softlens, melati, and selected traditional accessories according to makeup/attire tier.
- Free adat accessories are limited to Sunda, Betawi, Jawa, and Koto Gadang. Other adat accessories are additional/charged.
- Catering orders meeting the printed minimum may include ice cream/es goder, after-akad menu, snacks, VIP menu, decoration, waiter, and service charge.

## Common Exclusions

- Building deposit if required by venue.
- Pelaminan stage if the venue does not provide it.
- Venue fee, venue surcharge, and charge gedung unless a venue-inclusive package explicitly says otherwise.
