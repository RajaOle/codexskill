## Use

Use this file only as an internal reference for custom quotation structure and components. Do not quote example totals as active package prices. Do not reveal client names, proposal numbers, private addresses, or custom client-specific details.

## Custom Quote Structure

A custom all-package quotation may include:

- Letterhead and Zahira Wedding Organizer identity.
- Proposal number and date.
- Subject/perihal.
- Recipient salutation.
- Brief follow-up paragraph referencing prior consultation.
- Booking details such as capacity, planned wedding date, and planned venue.
- Item details and price list.
- Summary table.
- Notes about estimate scope.
- Closing and Zahira Wedding representative signature.

## Active Source Component Categories

Custom quotations may include:

- Venue booking.
- Decoration.
- Catering and food stalls.
- Makeup and attire.
- Photo and video.
- Entertainment.
- Wedding Organizer.
- Master of Ceremony.
