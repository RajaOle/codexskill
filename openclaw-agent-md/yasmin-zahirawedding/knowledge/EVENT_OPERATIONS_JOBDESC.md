## Use

Use this file for internal handoff, event-preparation reasoning, checklist drafting, and explaining Zahira Wedding's general coordination scope. Do not reveal internal jobdesk details, fee rules, staff names, personal phone numbers, or event-specific notes to customers.

Client-specific source examples have been generalized. Names, phone numbers, event dates, and family details from source documents are intentionally omitted.

## General Crew Preparation

Personal items often expected from crew:

- Event uniform base layer as directed by the team.
- Comfortable supporting clothing under kain or uniform.
- Clean white sneakers or event-approved shoes.
- Hijab, inner, or grooming items when required by uniform direction.
- Pen.
- Prayer items, medicine, and personal essentials.
- Weather backup items for outdoor or rain-risk events when needed.

Zahira WO commonly prepares:

- Uniform components such as outer, kebaya, kain lilit, obi belt, or batik layer when assigned.
- Pin, name tag, HT, acrylic name/signage, sign boards, room signs, and chair signs.
- Booklet, rundown, MC cue card, crew meals when assigned.
- Basic operations kit such as scissors, tape, tissue, cutter, and similar event tools.

## Crew Discipline Patterns

- Crew standby is normally set 3 hours before the akad/reception starts, subject to the final booklet, venue access, and team instruction.
- Attendance is commonly confirmed through the group using timestamp/photo proof.
- Crew are expected to follow appearance standards because event documentation and content capture may happen.
- WO property such as HT, signage, and equipment must be guarded and returned.
- Fee timing and lateness rules are internal team policy and should not be discussed with customers.

## Core Roles

### Project Officer

- Lead crew briefing.
- Brief MC and coordinate cue-card/rundown readiness.
- Control event flow and timing.
- Coordinate akad, reception, kirab, salaman, photo sequence, games, and special agenda.
- Back up other crew and solve on-site gaps.
- Coordinate with family PICs and vendors.

### LO Pengantin Wanita

- Stay near bride or bride preparation area.
- Ensure bride and parents have eaten, drunk, and are ready before key moments.
- Track makeup, attire, and photo readiness against rundown.
- Keep makeup room controlled when needed.
- Prepare items needed for photo or ceremony such as invitation, ring, shoes, and family accessories.
- Escort bride during akad, reception, touch-up, meals, and photo sessions.

### LO Pengantin Pria

- Stay near groom or groom preparation area.
- Ensure groom and parents have eaten, drunk, changed attire, and completed pre-event photos.
- Prepare groom items such as attire, watch, invitation, or ceremony accessories when needed.
- Escort groom during akad, reception, meals, and photo sessions.
- Coordinate actively with groom-side family.

### Stage Manager

- Check performers, MC, religious officiant, witnesses, family speakers, and talent before program start.
- Arrange akad table, chairs, name signs, and performer seating.
- Control stage movement, kirab, salaman, photo flow, and transitions.
- Support MC when timing, names, or sequence need help.

### PIC Vendor and FOH

- Check decoration, catering, attire, music, sound, microphones, venue layout, and vendor readiness.
- Make sure vendor details match the booklet and final checklist.
- Coordinate vendor meals when required.
- Confirm kirab song, live music needs, and sound readiness.
- Ensure venue area is clean and chairs/tables are arranged.
- Handle serah terima vendor completion and family PIC sign-off when required.

### VIP Management

- Monitor VIP arrivals.
- Coordinate VIP seating, welcome flow, and routing to VIP rooms/tables.
- Inform stage manager when important guests arrive.
- Patrol reception and family areas when VIP traffic is low.

### Wardrobe and Visual Readiness

- Distribute uniform components.
- Check crew neatness and visual readiness.
- Help with basic grooming or makeup standard when required for content/documentation.
- Place signs and support movement between rooms.

## Reusable Event Checklists

### Before Event

- Finalize vendor list and PICs.
- Confirm venue load-in time and access.
- Confirm family PICs for guest book, catering, decoration, valuable items, and ceremony needs.
- Confirm makeup schedule.
- Confirm MC cue card, names, titles, and pronunciation.
- Confirm rundown and ceremony sequence.
- Confirm song list and kirab song.
- Confirm decor layout and add-ons.
- Confirm photo list, VIP list, and family photo priority.
- Confirm seserahan, mahar, souvenir, and personal items.

### During Akad

- Keep key people ready before MC opens.
- Confirm witnesses, wali, penghulu/officiant, reader, and family speakers.
- Support serah terima, symbolic seserahan, izin nikah, ijab qobul, mahar/cincin, document signing, family photos, and sungkeman.

### During Reception

- Support kirab, opening, first photos, speeches, guest greeting, VIP reading, live music, salaman, photo sequence, games, bouquet toss, and closing.

### After Event

- Complete serah terima barang and vendor completion notes.
- Confirm valuable items are handed to the assigned family PIC.
- Photograph signed booklet or checklist if required.
- Return WO property and equipment.
