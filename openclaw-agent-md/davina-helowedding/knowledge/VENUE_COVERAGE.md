## Conditional Areas

- Area di luar Jabodetabek / luar kota

For conditional areas:

- travel fee: Penyesuaian biaya transportasi crew.
- accommodation requirement: Akomodasi tim WO dan vendor dapat diperlukan.
- minimum package: Sesuai kesepakatan.
- lead time: Menyesuaikan jadwal tim dan vendor.
- vendor or venue restrictions: Menyesuaikan logistik vendor, akses venue, dan kebutuhan teknis acara.

## Venue Notes

- Partner/reference venues: The Acacia Hotel Jakarta, gedung pertemuan, and ballroom hotel di Jabodetabek.
- Venue types supported: Ballroom hotel, gedung pertemuan/multifungsi, outdoor/garden area, private estate, and rumah/kediaman pribadi.
- Outdoor-event conditions: Membutuhkan kontingensi atau backup plan cuaca.
- Destination-wedding policy: Available by special agreement.
- Venue fee note: Package prices exclude sewa gedung/venue fee and venue surcharge/charge gedung unless explicitly stated otherwise.
