## Quotation and Price Conditions

- Quoted package prices exclude venue fee, sewa gedung, and charge gedung unless stated otherwise.
- Packages are customizable, subject to agreement with Helo Wedding.
- Final quotation depends on event date, venue, area, guest count, package choice, vendor logistics, add-ons, and any venue-specific charges.
- Davina may quote only prices listed in `PACKAGES_AND_PRICING.md`.

## Deposit Requirement

- DP I / Booking Fee: IDR 5,000,000 upon Service Order signing or to lock the event date.
- For bookings made within H-5 months before the event date, DP I is 50% of total contract value.
- Booking Fee / DP I is non-refundable.

## Payment Schedule

- DP I / Booking Fee: IDR 5,000,000 at contract signing or booking.
- Termin 1 / DP II: 50% of total dealing value at H-60 before event, or maximum 30 calendar days after DP I.
- Final settlement / pelunasan: remaining balance at H-14 before event or at Technical Meeting, whichever comes first.

## Cancellation Policy

- Client cancellation: DP I / Booking Fee of IDR 5,000,000 is non-refundable.
- Cancellation at least 90 days before H-Day: 40% refund of payments made after DP I, after deducting vendor/supplier down payments and non-refundable booking fees.
- Cancellation 2 months to 30 days before H-Day: refund is calculated after deducting operational expenses and vendor down payments.
- Cancellation 30 days or less before H-Day: 100% forfeited.
- Cancellation by Helo Wedding more than 30 days before event: 100% refund of all amounts paid.
- Cancellation by Helo Wedding less than 30 days before event: 100% refund plus 20% penalty fee to the client.

## Scope Modification and Vendor Substitution

- Package additions, reductions, substitutions, or scope modifications must be confirmed at least 30 days before H-Day.
- Decoration vendor cancellation by client: IDR 3,000,000 penalty.
- Other vendor cancellation by client, including MUA, attire, MC, and entertainment: up to IDR 3,000,000 per vendor.
- Budget from unused items may be reallocated to other items only by written mutual agreement.

## Meeting and Operations

- Family Meeting: 1x pre-event meeting per contract.
- Technical Meeting: 1x formal meeting with vendors, typically around H-14.
- Unlimited consultation via WhatsApp/phone is included for contracted clients.
