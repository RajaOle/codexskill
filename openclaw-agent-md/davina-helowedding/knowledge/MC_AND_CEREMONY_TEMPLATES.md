## Use

Use this file for drafting or summarizing ceremony flow, MC cue-card structure, sambutan, izin nikah, and lamaran references. Do not expose client-specific names, family details, private contacts, or source event details.

## MC Cue Card Structure

A practical MC cue card commonly contains:

- Event date, time, and venue.
- Groom data: full name, parents, sibling/order information if needed.
- Bride data: full name, parents, sibling/order information if needed.
- Ceremony contributors: officiant/penghulu, KUA, witnesses, wali nikah, speakers, Quran reader, saritilawah, prayer/nasihat, MC.
- Akad highlights.
- Reception highlights.
- Rundown table with time, program, performer, and PIC.
- Print note when physical cue card is needed.

## Akad Rundown Building Blocks

- Vendor and family preparation.
- Family arrival and formation.
- MC opening.
- Groom-side family speech and stated purpose.
- Bride-side family response.
- Symbolic seserahan or angsul-angsul.
- Groom reception by bride-side family where applicable.
- Family and guests seated.
- Formal akad opening.
- Calling witnesses, wali nikah, and penghulu/officiant.
- Quran reading if included.
- Bride's permission request to parents if included.
- Ijab qobul led by penghulu/officiant.
- Bride entrance or seating transition if part of concept.
- Nasihat pernikahan.
- Mahar, ring, document signing, and family photo.
- Sungkeman.
- MC closing akad.
- Family photos, beauty shoot, break, ramah tamah, and touch-up.

## Reception Rundown Building Blocks

- MC opening reception.
- Kirab pengantin and family procession.
- Initial family photo.
- Speech or sambutan.
- VIP greeting or VIP name reading when needed.
- Ramah tamah, salaman, and photo sessions.
- Live music.
- Games or bouquet toss when included.
- MC closing.

## Sambutan Template Types

### Bride-Side Family Acceptance

Purpose:

- Welcome groom-side family.
- Express gratitude and honor for the visit.
- Acknowledge union of two families.
- Accept the groom into the family with respect.
- Apologize for any shortcomings in hosting.
- Pray for sakinah, mawaddah, warahmah.

### Groom-Side Family Handover

Purpose:

- Greet bride-side family.
- Express gratitude for the occasion.
- State the family's intention to entrust the groom.
- Ask bride-side family to accept and guide the couple.
- Pray for blessing, patience, respect, and lifelong harmony.

### Bride Permission Before Ijab

Purpose:

- Ask parents for blessing and permission.
- Thank parents for care, sacrifice, prayer, and upbringing.
- Ask forgiveness for mistakes.
- State intention to begin married life.
- Ask for prayers and blessing.

### Parent Reply to Permission

Purpose:

- Acknowledge the child's request.
- Forgive and bless the child.
- Give permission for the marriage.
- Pray for protection, blessing, love, and good descendants.
- Remind the child to honor spouse and in-laws.

## Lamaran Rundown Building Blocks

- General preparation for family, venue, makeup, photographer, and vendors.
- MC opening and family readiness announcement.
- Arrival and seating of proposing-side family.
- Proposing-side family speech with intent.
- Receiving-side family response and family introduction.
- Couple's direct lamaran moment if included.
- Flower or symbolic gift moment if included.
- Ring exchange by mothers or family representatives if included.
- Symbolic hantaran exchange.
- Prayer and closing.
- Optional games, meal, ramah tamah, and photo session.

## Lamaran Script Pattern

Proposing side:

- Greet both families.
- State gratitude for the gathering.
- State sincere intention to ask permission and propose.
- Express commitment to care for and build a serious future with the partner.
- Ask directly whether the partner accepts the proposal.

Receiving side:

- Thank the proposing-side family for coming.
- Acknowledge the good intention.
- Express gratitude for the future family relationship.
- Answer the proposal clearly.
- Pray for ease and blessing toward marriage.
