## Reference Service Components

Use these components to summarize a proposed package or escalation:

- Decorations: pelaminan, kursi pelaminan, mini garden, standing flower, karpet jalan, wedding gate, welcome sign, set akad, janur, kotak angpau, meja penerima tamu, backdrop, hand bouquet, buku tamu, lighting/effect.
- Photo and video: edited photos, photographer, videographer, album, frames, highlight/cinematic video, master files.
- MC and entertainment: MC akad/resepsi, organ tunggal, singer, keyboardist, sound system, mic wireless, live band when included.
- Makeup and attire: makeup akad/resepsi, touch up, busana pengantin, family attire/makeup, traditional/adat add-ons when requested.
- Catering: buffet, gubukan, after-akad menu, catering decor when included.
- WO services: budgeting, coordination, vendor communication, rundown, wedding booklet, family meeting, technical meeting, WA group, project officer, crew on standby.
- Bonuses: seserahan box, digital invitation, standing photo or other package-specific bonuses.
